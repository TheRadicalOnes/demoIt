## Slides on top of your app

Every feature demoIt has, once each.

- It presents over any website, so you need no org to log into
- Edit `deck.md`, re-pick the folder on the options page, reload the tab
- Settings go at the top of a slide, above the heading

## Slides you never show

A slide can exist only as a cue. The HUD names it and lets you step past it while the application
keeps the screen, which is why the arrows are on the HUD rather than only in the deck.
